NERVE INTO PROGRESS

Crimes 2.0

Crimes turn a bar you'd otherwise waste into money, items, and the crime experience that powers Organized Crimes. Here's how the system works, how to grow it safely, and what each crime is actually good for.

N
Nico "The Line"Word on the Street

"Nerve's just money you haven't collected yet. Spend it before it caps — but spend it on crimes you can actually pull off. Get greedy early and you'll pay for it."

The system

How crimes work

Two things you earn

  • CS
    Crime Skill — a level with a progress bar, tracked per crime. It's the number you watch climb as you specialise.
  • CE
    Crime Experience — hidden, shared across all crimes. It sets your Natural Nerve Bar and feeds Organized Crime success. This is the one that matters faction-wide.

Three outcomes

  • ✓
    Success — cash and/or items, plus CE and CS.
  • –
    Fail (orange) — no reward, but no loss either.
  • ✗
    Critical fail (red) — lose CE and CS (can drop a whole level), plus jail, hospital, or lost items/cash.

Every crime costs Nerve. Your nerve bar refills over time — the whole game is spending it on the right crime before it sits full.

Grow it safely

Build your nerve (CE)

Your Natural Nerve Bar rises in steps of 5 as your hidden CE crosses thresholds — and each step gets harder to reach. Higher CE also raises your success chance and unlocks harder crimes.

R
RookFaction Intel

"CE isn't just your nerve bar — it's what makes you useful in an Organized Crime. Build it steady now and you're an asset when the faction needs bodies for an OC. Waste your bar and you're dead weight."

The one rule: don't over-reach

A critical fail cuts your CE by a percentage, not a flat amount — one or two bad results on a high-nerve crime can erase days of progress. Only move up to a harder crime as your natural nerve bar rises. When in doubt, grind the crime you pass reliably.

Raise your success rate

  • +2%
    Buy the crime's enhancer item early — you'll do thousands of attempts, so 2% is a lot of extra successes.
  • ☺
    Happiness and merits both help — the same happy that boosts gym gains helps crimes.
  • 🎓
    Bachelor of Psychological Sciences (education) and the Federal Judge rank in the Law city job.
  • ⚑
    The faction Criminality upgrade tree — a faction-wide boost worth pushing for.

Why your grind matters to everyone

Your crimes power the faction's OCs

Individual crimes aren't just personal income — they're the fuel for Organized Crimes (OC 2.0), one of a faction's biggest sources of cash, items and respect. The whole thing runs on a chain that starts with you doing crimes.

The chain

do crimes → raise CE → raise NNB → higher CPR → OC succeeds → faction cash · items · respect · Scope

Your CPR (Checkpoint Pass Rate) — your success chance in an OC role — is driven mainly by your Natural Nerve Bar, and NNB only rises by committing crimes. No crime grind, no NNB, no CPR: you become the slot that drags the whole crew's payout down.

R
RookFaction Intel

"An OC is only as strong as its weakest slot. Every crime you grind on a quiet day is you keeping your CPR up so you don't cost the crew a payout. Skip it and you're the reason we fail — and everyone sees whose number was red."

Roles & CPR

  • %
    An OC has slots (roles). Each open slot shows a coloured success chance for you — that's your CPR for that role.
  • NNB
    CPR rises mainly with your NNB (crimes), plus the role's required item. Put people in roles they actually pass.
  • ?
    There's a hidden factor too — an OC can occasionally clear even with a lower score — but don't plan around luck.

Tiers & rewards

  • 1–10
    OCs run in difficulty tiers. Higher tier = higher CPR required, but ~double the rewards of the tier below (cash, items, respect).
  • ↑
    Building NNB is what unlocks the bigger, better-paying OC roles. Your personal grind is the faction's access to the top tiers.

Scope — the faction's fuel

  • ⛽
    Spawning an OC costs Scope. A faction starts at 25, caps at 100, and it only regenerates +1/day.
  • +
    You earn 1 + the OC's tier in Scope for each one you clear — so successes fund more OCs; failures burn Scope for nothing.
  • ✓
    That's why members' readiness matters faction-wide: the faction should only run OCs its people can pass, or it bleeds Scope.

Boost OC success

  • ⚑
    The same crime boosters carry over: happy, merits, education, the faction Criminality tree.
  • 🏢
    A Law Firm company special boosts OC success — handy if a member works there.
  • 🎒
    Bring the role's required item — a missing item can block an otherwise-ready member (this is where the faction armory earns its keep).

What it means for staff

Match each member to the highest role they reliably pass, make sure they have the required item (loan from the armory if not), and time slot-filling to when people finish their current OC. Don't leave a high-NNB member sitting in a low role — that's value left on the table. (This is exactly what the faction bot's OC optimizer is built to automate.)

Pick your lane

The crimes

Twelve crimes across six categories (more on the way). Some are money, some are pure skill/CE, some drop items, some are time-gated. Specialise, but keep your CE climbing.

Theft
Search for CashMoney

The starter. Search spots (Trash, Subway, Junkyard; unlock Beach, Cemetery, Fountain) for cash and items. Your bread-and-butter early earner and CE builder.

ShopliftingItems

Lift goods from shops — a source of candy, jewelry and rings (yes, wedding rings). Learn each shop's drop rates.

PickpocketingSkill

Fast and low-risk, but light on income. Great for grinding CS/CE without much downside.

BurglaryMoney

Break into houses — scout first, mind the drop tables and safety. Higher-value hauls once you can handle it.

Counterfeiting
BootleggingMoney

Copy and sell media on a timer — a steady, semi-passive earner once your setup is running.

ForgeryItems

Forge documents (ID badges, ATM keys) from material components on timers, with fixed item outputs. More parts to juggle than Bootlegging.

Vandalism
GraffitiSkill

Tag locations with color/location mechanics — a low-risk CS/CE builder.

ArsonSkill

Newer vandalism crime — burn targets. Note it can hospitalize you rather than jail you.

Fraud
Card SkimmingMoney

Skim card data from machines on time-dependent windows. Fail and you can lose your card details — timing matters.

HustlingMoney

Street cons and hustles for cash.

ScammingMoney

The most complex crime yet — work the mark and persuasion bars; even player age factors in. High skill ceiling.

Illicit Services
DisposalTime-gated

A daily-job system — the most time-gated crime out. Patience is the whole game here.

Cybercrime
CrackingSkill

Crack passwords, Wordle-style — a mix of Disposal and Pickpocketing. Puzzle-brained players do well here.

The play

What to actually do

  • 1
    Never sit on a full nerve bar — that's wasted CE and cash, same as capped energy is wasted stats.
  • 2
    Grind the hardest crime you pass reliably, not the hardest one available. Move up only as your natural nerve bar rises.
  • 3
    Buy the enhancer for your main crime immediately (+2% over thousands of attempts).
  • 4
    Money now vs. CE for later: chase the money crimes (Search for Cash, Bootlegging, Burglary, Card Skimming, Scamming) when you need cash; grind the safe ones to bank CE for OCs.
  • 5
    Stack success boosters — happy, merits, education, Law job, faction Criminality tree.
